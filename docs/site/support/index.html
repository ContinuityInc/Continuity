Continuity Support

A local music player with DJ-style blends

Importing music

Tap + and pick audio files (or a folder) from the Files app. Tracks land in Local Files. Tempo, key, and loudness are analysed on the device; stem separation for vocal-aware blends also runs on-device the first time a track needs it.

No account

Continuity does not have sign-in. There is no username or password. Your library never leaves the phone.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/ContinuityInc/Continuity/issues. Include the iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong.
